# Ecommerce Sales Dashboard | YTD Sales Analysis

## Project Overview

This Tableau dashboard provides an interactive analysis of Year-to-Date (YTD) sales performance for an e-commerce business. It enables users to monitor key business metrics, evaluate category performance, analyze market contribution, and track customer acquisition trends across different regions.

## Key Metrics

* **YTD Sales:** $14.11M (+5.04% vs Previous Year)
* **YTD Profit:** $1.30M (-0.46% vs Previous Year)
* **YTD Order Quantity:** 157.98K (+15.02% vs Previous Year)

## Dashboard Features

### 1. KPI Overview

Displays YTD Sales, Profit, and Order Quantity with:

* Comparison against Previous Year (PY)
* Year-over-Year (YoY) performance indicators
* Monthly sales trend sparklines

### 2. Market Share Analysis

Visualizes sales contribution by market:

* LATAM: 45.86%
* Europe: 43.37%
* Pacific Asia: 8.32%
* Africa: 2.25%
* USCA: 0.21%

### 3. Category Performance

Compares YTD Sales against Previous Year To Date (PYTD) across product categories.

* Green: Better than previous year
* Red: Lower than previous year
* Blue: No sales recorded in previous year

### 4. Customer Acquisition Analysis

Tracks monthly customer acquisition performance across different markets:

* Africa
* Europe
* LATAM
* Pacific Asia
* USCA

### 5. Interactive Filters

Users can dynamically analyze results by:

* Customer Segment
* Market

## Business Insights

* Sales increased by 5.04% compared with the previous year.
* Order quantity grew significantly, indicating stronger demand.
* Profit slightly declined despite higher sales volume, suggesting margin pressure.
* LATAM and Europe contributed the majority of total sales.
* Camping & Hiking and Water Sports demonstrated strong YoY growth.

## Tools Used

* Tableau Public
* Level of Detail (LOD) Expressions
* Calculated Fields
* Dashboard Actions & Filters
* Year-to-Date (YTD) Analysis
